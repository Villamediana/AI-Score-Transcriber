About

AI Score Transcriber listens to audio and writes down what it hears: a piano roll you can watch, a score you can edit, and a MIDI file you can take anywhere.

It works with any instrument and with several notes at once. Piano and solo instruments come out cleanest; dense mixes with drums and vocals are the hardest.

Every transcription gets its own link, and your edits save into it as you make them. Copy it from Share to pick the work up on another device, or to send it to someone. The score keeps working for good; the original audio it was made from is kept for two weeks.
